Music & Learning

Help every singer arrive prepared.

Your music library should do more than store PDFs. Choir Concierge gives singers the tools they need to learn their parts, practise at their own pace and prepare for performance, all in one place.

  • Sheet music and learning tracks: Keep scores, demo recordings and part-learning tracks together with each song.

  • Practise your way: Read sheet music while listening to audio, adjust tempo and pitch, control volume and pan, or jump back a few seconds to practise a tricky passage.

  • Find your pitch: Use the built-in pitch pipe and chromatic scale to help singers find their starting notes.

  • Track learning progress: Singers can mark songs they're learning or ready to be assessed on, while music leaders can track progress and mark songs performance-ready.

  • Keep repertoire organised: Manage songs as they move from preparation to learning, active repertoire and archive.

The audio player keeps playing as you move around the app, so you can keep listening while checking another song or reviewing other choir information.

Whether you're learning your first concert piece or polishing a competition set, your music is ready when you are.

Events & Attendance

Keep every event running smoothly.

Rehearsals, performances, workshops, retreats and social events all need coordination. Choir Concierge makes it easier to organise what's coming up and keep track of who's attending.

  • Plan events: Set dates, times, locations, arrival times, descriptions and schedules, including the songs planned for each event.

  • Collect RSVPs: See who's going, who's unsure and who hasn't responded yet. Review useful planning details such as dietary requirements and medical information.

  • Record attendance: Mark attendance individually or in bulk, with options for late arrivals and absence reasons.

  • Make check-in easier: Use a shared-device kiosk or a QR code for singers to check themselves in.

  • Sync your calendar: Add individual events to your calendar or subscribe to a choir calendar feed.

  • Understand attendance: Review attendance reports across a period, compare participation and identify changes in engagement over time.

From the weekly rehearsal to the big performance, spend less time chasing responses and more time getting the choir ready.

Membership

Know your people. Keep everything organised.

Your choir is more than a list of names. Choir Concierge keeps singer information, membership history and practical administrative details together, so the people looking after the choir can find what they need.

  • Rich singer profiles: Store contact details, emergency contacts, membership information, voice placement and other relevant details.

  • Flexible membership statuses: Keep track of regular members, inactive members, prospects and former members.

  • Voice parts and ensembles: Record each singer's voice part, even if they sing a different part in each ensemble in your organisation.

  • Custom fields: Capture the information that matters to your choir, without being limited to a fixed set of fields.

  • Membership fee tracking: Keep an eye on fee due dates and see who's paid, approaching expiry or overdue.

  • Import and export: Import singer information from supported choir-management systems or a CSV template, and export records when needed.

  • Preserve membership history: Retain historical records as singers move through different stages of membership.

Communications

Choir-wide communication, without the chaos.

Important messages shouldn't disappear into a maze of inboxes, group chats and forwarded emails. Choir Concierge connects your choir's email communication with the people and groups it needs to reach.

Keep using your usual email client.

Choir mailing lists let you send messages from your own email account, while Choir Concierge handles distribution to the list's recipients and records the message in the system.

  • Mailing lists for every purpose: Set up lists for the whole choir, individual ensembles, leadership teams, music teams or other groups.

  • Reach the right people: Target recipients and senders by singer, role, voice part, membership status or ensemble.

  • Choose how conversations work: Use chat-style lists for group conversations, mailouts for announcements, or public lists for designated contact addresses.

  • Send polished broadcasts: Compose announcements in Choir Concierge with rich-text formatting and attachments, and they'll automatically include your logo.

  • Keep a central record: Review sent messages, inbound emails, distribution activity and message opens.

Whether you're coordinating a committee, announcing a new piece of repertoire or sending information to the whole choir, communication stays connected to your organisation.

Single Login & Ensembles

One account. Multiple groups. Less juggling.

Choirs don't always operate as a single, uniform group. Singers may belong to several organisations, while a single organisation might contain multiple ensembles with different members, music and activities.

Choir Concierge is designed to accommodate both.

One login across multiple choirs.

Use the same account to access multiple organisations, without juggling separate usernames and passwords.

  • Switch between choirs easily: Move between organisations from within the app.
  • Choose your default choir: Go straight to your preferred workspace when you log in.
  • Keep one account: Use the same login whether you're a singer, director or administrator across multiple organisations.

Multiple groups within one organisation.

Organise your choir into ensembles, sub-groups or other musical groups, each with its own singers, repertoire and events.

  • Flexible membership: Enrol singers in one or more ensembles, with a different voice part for each.
  • Relevant music and events: Assign songs, events and other resources to the ensembles that need them.
  • Group-specific access: Singers see items assigned to their ensembles, while items without an ensemble restriction remain available across the organisation.
  • Easy to manage: Add, edit or remove ensembles and give each its own name and logo.

The result: a setup that works for a single choir, a choir with sub-groups, or people involved in multiple organisations, without forcing everyone into the same structure.
